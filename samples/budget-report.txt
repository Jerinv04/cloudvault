Q3 Cloud Infrastructure Budget Report

Summary: AWS spend this quarter stayed under the allocated budget thanks to
serverless architecture - Lambda, DynamoDB on-demand and S3 only bill for
actual usage. Rekognition and Comprehend costs were the largest AI line item,
driven mainly by image tagging volume. KMS key rotation and CloudWatch
logging added a small fixed cost. Recommendation: keep the current
architecture for the next quarter and monitor the sensitive-file detection
rate as a proxy for data governance.
